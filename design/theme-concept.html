Spectral Plate

The interface works like an infrared survey instrument. Every panel is a photographic plate with registration marks. The accent colour is the wavelength of the image you're looking at, taken from SPHEREx's own six detector bands. Corners step in pixels, so Voyager, our pixel-art guide, lives in the same world as the UI.

Five ideas

01

Wavelength is the accent

No fixed brand colour. The accent is the band of the image on screen, so the interface tells you what light you're looking at.

02

Plates, not cards

Panels are survey plates: warm off-black emulsion, corner registration marks, a plate ID line and a strip of the filter's spectrum.

03

Pixel-stepped corners

Corners step down in 2 px stairs instead of rounding. The detector is made of pixels, and so is Voyager.

04

Develop, don't fade

Panels appear like a plate developing: a short grain-to-clear reveal over 250 ms. Reduced-motion users see it instantly.

05

Telemetry voice

Voyager speaks in cream "transmission" slips with a typing cursor. The interface itself stays quiet and lets the sky lead.

Palette

The six band colours run short to long wavelength (violet to red) in infrared false colour, the convention astronomers use. Base tones are warm, like photographic emulsion, rather than the usual cold blue-black.
