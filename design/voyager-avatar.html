Voyager

Three refined directions. All keep Voyager's DNA: a radio dish, gold foil, one amber eye and a pointing boom. They're drawn at 32 px instead of 16, with three-tone shading, an amber key light from the top left, a blue rim light from the right, and dark outlines so they read against any sky. Deadpan humour lives in the eye, so each one comes with a set of expressions.

The current 16 × 13 sprite. The idea works, but at this size there's no room for expressions or shading, and it turns into a blob against busy parts of the sky.

Three directions

Click an expression to try it on the big sprite. "Point" extends the boom; "Arrive" plays the landing squash.

What I'd fix in every direction

  • Silhouette first. Each design should be recognisable as a solid black shape at 16 px, so it reads mid-flight and in the intro.
  • The eye carries the joke. Deadpan is a flat bar; sarcasm is a half-closed lid; surprise is a wide square. No mouth, so it never turns cartoony.
  • Two lights, always. Amber from the top left (the interface's signal colour) and blue rim light from the right (the glint colour), so Voyager always looks like it belongs in Endurance Deck.
  • Squash and stretch on the body only. The dish and eye keep their shape so the face stays readable while the body squashes.
