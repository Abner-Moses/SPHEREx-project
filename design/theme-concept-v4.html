The same three "very crazy" moments as v3, now quieter and more cinematic: the boot is a slow systems check, the beam is softer, and the uplink keeps its hex stream.

What changed from 03

01

Brackets mean focus

The blue corners are gone from every panel except the one in focus. Panels are now flat slabs with a single top rule and a spaced-out label.

02

Wide capitals

Michroma, a wide 1970s-style face with very open spacing, replaces the dot matrix for names and labels. Dot matrix survives only in the clocks.

03

Two clocks, one gap

"Sky epoch" (the date the stars are drawn at) and "your time", with the difference between them in red. It's a real feature of the app, framed like time dilation.

04

Instruments, not widgets

An attitude dial shows where you're looking and how wide. View switches are lamp toggles. Data reads like a flight log.

05

Film, not screen

Fixed film grain (it doesn't flicker), a soft vignette and cream label text give the warmth of a projected film instead of a monitor.

06

Voyager on a carrier line

Its messages hang off a single amber edge line instead of a boxed panel, like a caption on a cockpit display.
